# spe

orchestration job-managers workers

Project for task orchestration with job managers, workers and scheduling.

Systems work on coordination: distributing tasks, managing lifecycles, and handling scheduling concerns across job managers and worker pools. The focus is on reliability and clear separation of concerns in a concurrent environment.

Concepts

  • ›Job managers that own queues and state.
  • ›Workers that pull and execute tasks.
  • ›Orchestration layer tying scheduling and supervision together.
