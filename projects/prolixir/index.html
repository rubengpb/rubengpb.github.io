# prolixir BSc thesis · Elixir ↔ Ciao Prolog

BSc thesis elixir ciao-prolog processes

Library to interact with Ciao Prolog from Elixir through OS processes. Part of my BSc thesis (TFG).

The BEAM and Prolog worlds meet via process communication: Elixir spawns and talks to a Ciao Prolog engine, exchanging queries and answers. Useful for embedding logic programming inside Elixir applications without tight native bindings.

What it does

  • ›Spawns Ciao Prolog as a managed OS process from Elixir.
  • ›Sends queries and streams results back to Elixir.
  • ›Keeps the boundary simple: processes and messages, no NIFs.

Context

Built during my undergraduate studies (TFG) while exploring the intersection of logic and functional/concurrent programming.
